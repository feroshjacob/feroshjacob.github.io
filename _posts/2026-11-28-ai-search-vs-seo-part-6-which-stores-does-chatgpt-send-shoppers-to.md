## The short version

In this test, ChatGPT sent shoppers to stores, and never to Amazon. I typed the eight product searches from [Part 4](https://feroshjacob.github.io/posts/2026/11/08/ai-search-vs-seo-part-4-ask-for-a-product-google-shows-stores-claude-reads-reviews) into ChatGPT on three evenings. Claude had answered with reviews. ChatGPT answered with product cards that link out: to Walmart, Home Depot, Best Buy and the maker's own site. Not one of its 92 links went to Amazon, the store Google put in its top five on 18 of 24 pages.

## Where does ChatGPT send shoppers?

Of the 92 links ChatGPT gave for the eight product searches, 56 went to stores and 28 to the maker's own site. Only 2 went to a review site. Walmart (12 links), Home Depot (12) and Best Buy (11) led.

ChatGPT's text named Amazon as a store in 3 of 27 answers, always in a list of several. It never linked to Amazon, and Amazon was not among the 202 source links it listed.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amazon | 18 | 10 | 0 | 0 |
| Big-box chains (Walmart, Best Buy, Home Depot…) | 18 | 27 | 1 | 38 |
| Other stores | 18 | 14 | 31 | 18 |

Google and Bing count every top-five link; Claude each site once per answer; ChatGPT every link. Its 6 "other" links reopened ChatGPT.

## It cites reviewers, but links to stores

Asked to list its sources, ChatGPT gave 102 sites for the eight searches, counting a site once per answer; 40 were review, news or blog sites. Its links went to review sites twice. The reviewers get the citation; the store gets the click.

## The store on the card is not always the store you get

63 links named a store on the card or its button. 16 went somewhere else: 12 to another store or to the maker, and 4 to a new ChatGPT search. A card marked "Target" opened Crate & Barrel. Two cards saying "Available at Lowe's" opened Home Depot. A "$469 Woot listing" opened nintendo.com. In all, 6 "View mattress" buttons opened a ChatGPT search; 4 of them are among the 16.

## Is it the same every evening?

Less so than search. With the same overlap score as Part 4 (1.0 means the same sites every evening), the sites ChatGPT linked scored 0.33, against 0.71 for Google's top five and 0.70 for Bing's. Part of that is the layout itself: on the first evening only 5 of 9 answers had links; on the next two, all 9 did. On the five searches with links every evening, the score was 0.43.

## Does it agree with Google, Bing and Claude?

Half the time. Across the eight searches ChatGPT linked 36 store or maker sites. 18 of them were in Google's or Bing's top five, or among Claude's stores, for the same search; 18 were in none. Ace Hardware and Grainger for drills were ChatGPT's alone.

**Local stays local.** For "tires near me", ChatGPT placed me in Marietta without asking, drew its own map, and its sources were mostly local tire shops, as Claude's were in Part 4.

## The limits

ChatGPT ran eleven to twelve days after Part 4's Google, Bing and Claude runs, so prices and stock differ; I compare stores and store types, not products. One ChatGPT answer per search per evening (27), against Claude's 81. Free plan, signed in, a temporary chat with web search on and memory on. On three answers the cards opened a side panel of stores instead of a link; I recorded the first store listed (14 of the 92 links). The "tires near me" map is ChatGPT's own (Mapbox), not Google's map pack. Store categories are my own sorting; method as in [Part 4's "How I measured"](https://feroshjacob.github.io/posts/2026/11/08/ai-search-vs-seo-part-4-ask-for-a-product-google-shows-stores-claude-reads-reviews#how-i-measured). Eight searches show a direction, not a law.

## What can a small seller do?

- **If you make the product, sell it on your own site.** 28 of ChatGPT's 92 links went to the maker.
- **Don't count on Amazon alone.** None of ChatGPT's 92 links went there.
- **Keep earning reviews.** 40 of the 102 sites ChatGPT cited were reviewers.
- **Get your products into ChatGPT.** As [Part 5](https://feroshjacob.github.io/posts/2026/11/15/ai-search-vs-seo-part-5-how-can-i-sell-products-through-chatgpt) found, Shopify and Etsy catalogs are already connected; other sellers apply at [chatgpt.com/merchants](https://chatgpt.com/merchants/).
- **Search for your own products in ChatGPT** and click through: 16 of 63 store-named links went elsewhere.
