Part 4 found that an AI assistant asked for a product reads reviews, not stores. The next question is the seller's: how do I actually sell through ChatGPT?

You can be found in ChatGPT today, but the sale happens on your own site. If you sell on Shopify or Etsy, your catalog is already connected. Everyone else applies at chatgpt.com/merchants. OpenAI says there are no fees on purchases that start in ChatGPT.

Google already gives sellers more for free than most use. Free listings through Merchant Center can appear on Search, Maps, Gemini and YouTube, and Google is further along on letting an AI agent check out for the shopper.

On the retailer side, BCG found 68% expect agentic commerce to have a significant or transformational effect within two years, and losing the customer relationship is their most common concern, cited by 91%.

For a small seller this week: turn on free listings, clean your product feed, and check your product data is accurate.
