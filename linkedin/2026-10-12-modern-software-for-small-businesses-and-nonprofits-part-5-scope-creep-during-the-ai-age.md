AI agents made it cheap to start things. Keeping track of them is another story.

On one client project, my Claude Code had 22 questions after the first release and 13 more after the second. By release 4 there were around 100 things going on. In my earlier career, 25 open items would have meant a dedicated PM and three or four teams.

On my own SEO tool, the agents built an app I didn't fully know how to use. When a button did nothing, I couldn't tell whether it was broken or not supposed to work yet.

I don't have a fix. Naming the problem is the first step. Part 5 of Modern Software for Small Businesses and Nonprofits is about scope creep in the AI age, with two thoughts on what comes next: agents negotiating with other agents, and doing fewer projects so we can stay in the details.
