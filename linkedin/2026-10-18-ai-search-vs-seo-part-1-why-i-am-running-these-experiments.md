For twenty years, being found meant ranking on Google. Now customers ask a chatbot, and nobody can tell a business owner why they are on the list.

The agents may one day be the customers that stores rank for. This series assumes humans stay the customers a little longer.

The agents already know what we need. The stores do not, and today there is no channel to tell them.

My hypothesis: a #1 Google rank does not guarantee a #1 ChatGPT rank. I am going to test it, and publish the results either way.

Part 1 of AI Search vs SEO is the why. Part 2 compares ChatGPT with Google and Bing.
