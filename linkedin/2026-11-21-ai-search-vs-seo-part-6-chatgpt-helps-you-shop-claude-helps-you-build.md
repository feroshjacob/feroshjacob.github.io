ChatGPT helps you shop. Claude helps you build.

I ran the eight product searches from Part 4 in ChatGPT on three evenings. ChatGPT answered with product cards that link out: 56 of its 92 links went to stores, 28 to the maker's own site, 2 to review sites. Zero went to Amazon, the store Google put in its top five on 18 of 24 pages.

In Part 4, Claude read reviews instead: 313 of the 367 sites it opened. And one report says Anthropic has open-sourced a blueprint for shopping agents that run on the merchant's own site, with checkout staying with the merchant. My framing, not a finding: one sends shoppers to stores, the other is something a store can build with.

One catch: 16 of the 63 ChatGPT links that named a store went somewhere else. Search for your own products and click through.
