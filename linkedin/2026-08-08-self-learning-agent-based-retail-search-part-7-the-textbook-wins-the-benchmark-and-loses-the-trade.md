Most retail search we built years ago — and most we build today — has the same layers: query understanding, then a retrieval engine with field weighting, supplemented by learning-to-rank.

The system the agents arrived at in this experiment had none of that. Just a lean hybrid.

So the natural question: what if I ask the agents to add those classic layers back — does it move the numbers?

It does. The full textbook stack was the single best configuration I measured.

By +0.13% over what is already shipped — a whole training pipeline, forever, for a tenth of a percent.

Is that good enough to promote to prod?
