"AI answers are random. Google is dependable." I believed that too, until I asked the same eight "near me" questions on three evenings.

Google's map box changed its line-up from night to night. ChatGPT and Claude changed too, about as much, not more. The steadiest thing I measured was Google's plain list of blue links.

That also means a correction to my own Part 2, where I said the map moved more than the chatbot. Counted fairly, they were about as steady as each other. I have added a dated note there.
