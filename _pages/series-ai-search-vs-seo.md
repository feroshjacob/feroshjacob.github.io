## Why I am running these experiments

For years, a business owner who wanted to be found had one main online lever: pay for SEO and try to rank on Google. It was expensive, but you could see the results page, check your position, and argue with the agency about it.

Now customers ask an AI assistant the same question and get a short list of businesses back. Nobody can audit that list. The assistant does not publish how it chose, and the same question can come back with a different answer tomorrow.

I would rather measure than guess. This series is a set of experiments: the same questions, asked repeatedly, across AI assistants and search engines, with the results published whether or not they flatter SEO. I assume throughout that businesses and products continue to rank for human readers, even as the agents learn more about us than the stores do.
