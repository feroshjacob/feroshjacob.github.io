Two months into [Northvalley Intelligence](https://northvalleyintel.com), I shared what those first months taught me: small businesses often run on paid but siloed systems — a spreadsheet here, a notebook there, a POS that never talks to the calendar — and the real win isn't a new website, it's connecting them around how the business actually works. I walked through three client stories: an insurance agency's manual carrier checks became an automated retention board that surfaces the policies to work; a mortgage broker's dream that had lived in an Excel sheet became a live app and site with instant pre-approval; and a tea shop's website that already hosted and sold her events gained scan-to-reserve seating.
