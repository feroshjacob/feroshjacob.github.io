A 3-minute lightning talk with Jiho Noh (Kennesaw State University) accompanying our accepted poster, presenting a reproducible CPU/GPU benchmark of search-relevance judges: one framework, five judges, two datasets, run identically on a laptop CPU and a GPU. The finding — nothing breaks 0.5 QWK, on a laptop or a GPU — suggests the quality ceiling for judging search relevance is set by the task itself, not the size of the model or the hardware behind it. This talk continues the [CPU vs GPU Battle](/series/cpu-vs-gpu-battle/) series on this site.
