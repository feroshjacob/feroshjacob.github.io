My second Paulding Connect talk followed up on September's with one local product we have been building at [Northvalley Intelligence](https://northvalleyintel.com): [Smart Cash Sheet](https://smartcashsheet.com), a personal-finance app built here in Paulding. I walked through four features: a dashboard with calendar projections, so shortages show up before they happen; the Cash Smart Score and mood tracking on any transaction; Segio, the AI money coach; and goals, debt tracking and what-if scenarios. I closed with a live demo of Smart Cash Sheet working inside ChatGPT.
